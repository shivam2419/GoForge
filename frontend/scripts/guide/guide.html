Step 2: ask your college these six questions

  1. How long? How many weeks or hours must the internship be, and does training time count?
  2. When? Which dates must it fall between, and when is the report or letter due?
  3. Online? Is an online (remote) internship accepted, or must it be on-site?
  4. Paperwork first? Do you need an NOC, or an offer letter before you start?
  5. Format? Is there a college format for the letter, the weekly diary or the final report?
  6. Which company? Must it be a registered company? Any other conditions?

We do not promise jobs or placements. What you get is training, a supervised project, and documents that can be checked.
