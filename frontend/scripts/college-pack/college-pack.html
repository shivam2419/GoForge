How a college partnership works

Six formats, one clear process.

1-day to 4-week hands-on sessions in AI, data, web or marketing.

Faculty development

Practical FDPs updating faculty on modern industry workflows and tools.

Bulk batch training

Live cohort training for a class or department aligned to your calendar.

Internship cohorts

3- or 6-month training + internship with verifiable completion letters.

Guest talks & expos

Practitioner sessions, hackathons and student project evaluations.
